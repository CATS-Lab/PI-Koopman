# Third-party data and software

OpenACC is provided by the European Commission Joint Research Centre under
CC BY 4.0. The AstaZero and Casale copyright notices are retained in
[data/external/openacc/](data/external/openacc/README.md). The episode manifest
selects 30-s windows; the OpenACC timing arrays contain relative states and inputs
derived from these windows. Cite Makridis et al. (2021),
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trc.2021.103047 and the JRC database,
https://doi.org/10.2905/JRC.KMH3D00.

NumPy, SciPy, pandas, scikit-learn, PyTorch, and threadpoolctl are installed as
external dependencies and retain their respective licenses. No dependency source
or binary distribution is bundled. The Deep Koopman baseline is this project's
adaptation, implemented in the included Python modules.
